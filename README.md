## Research & Engineering Focus

- **RF DSP & Software-Defined Radio (SDR)** — Wideband real-time channelizers, OFDM / FHSS / LTE physical-layer receivers, blind synchronization, MIMO STBC equalization, and RF emitter fingerprinting (`UHD`, `USRP`, `Sidekiq`).
- **GPU Computing & High-Performance C++/CUDA** — Low-latency zero-copy streaming pipelines, custom CUDA kernels, batched `cuFFT` filterbanks, and deterministic real-time scheduling on embedded **NVIDIA Jetson Orin** (`Nano / NX / AGX`).
- **Computational Mathematics & Signal Processing** — Fast Fourier transforms, spectral estimation, numerical verification, and sub-sample time/frequency recovery.
- **Applied AI & Multimodal Systems** — Open-world speaker discovery & voice biometrics, local hybrid vector/FTS5 search engines, and autonomous multi-agent simulation environments.
